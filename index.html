Why does it matter to human health?

Environmental health and human health are closely connected.

Poorly managed waste can contribute to air, water, and soil pollution, affecting both ecosystems and communities.

Pollution pathway

Air Pollution

When waste is burned improperly or decomposes in landfill, pollutants and greenhouse gases can be released into the air. These emissions can worsen local air quality and contribute to respiratory and cardiovascular health risks, especially for communities located near waste-handling facilities.

❤️
Human Health Impact 13 million

According to the World Health Organization, environmental factors such as air pollution, water contamination, and chemical exposure contribute to an estimated 13 million deaths globally each year.

While waste is only one of many environmental factors, responsible waste management plays an important role in reducing pollution and protecting community health.

Effective waste management helps reduce pollution and supports healthier environments for the people who live and work in them.

For organizations in the healthcare and life sciences industries, environmental health and human health are closely connected.
